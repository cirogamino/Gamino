Before you press record

The passes — inside

Slow, heel-to-toe. Each frame should overlap its neighbour by 70–80%. If you feel silly at how slow you're going, it's right.

The passes — outside

How much

Typical room200–350 frames
Large open space400–600 frames
Building exterior150–250 frames
Frame overlap70–80%

Shooting 4K video and letting the app pull frames is easier than taking hundreds of stills. A slow 3–4 minute walk gives plenty.

What kills a capture

Moving fast

Motion blur is the number one cause of a mushy result — more than the software you pick.

Auto-exposure left on

The brightness shifts frame to frame and the solver cannot line the shots up.

Anything that moved

A person walking through, a door swinging, a screen that changed. Freeze the room.

Then send me one file

The .PLY

Process the capture in Luma (free — try this first) or Polycam (PLY export needs Pro). Export as Gaussian Splat → PLY. That single file is all I need; I'll compress it, build the viewer, and drop your AI cast into your real room.

Do the inside first. One good room beats four rushed ones, and it proves the whole pipeline before you spend a weekend on the building.
