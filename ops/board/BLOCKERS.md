# BLOCKERS — append only

The most valuable file in this repo.

Every entry is a place where the world required a human. The list of blockers
from each run is the exact to-do list for making the *next* run more autonomous.
Ciro clears them once, permanently, and the ceiling rises.

Rule: log it, route around it, keep working. Never idle waiting on a blocker.

Format:

```
--- BLK-<n> | <SEAT> | H<hour> ---
WALL:      <exactly what stopped you — the URL and what it asked for>
TRIED:     <what you attempted, max 2 attempts>
ASK:       <the single concrete action Ciro takes, in under 5 minutes>
ROUTED TO: <what you did instead>
STATUS:    OPEN | CLEARED
```

---

## Known permanent walls (never attempt to bypass — Law 10)

- Phone / SMS verification
- Government ID upload
- Bank account linkage, SSN/EIN
- Captcha and bot checks
- 2FA challenges
- Any "confirm you are a human" gate
- Contracts and paid-tier terms above the cap

Hitting one of these is a **clean stop, log, and route**. It is never a puzzle to
solve. Circumventing an identity or bot check violates platform terms and is a
hard boundary, not a slow path.
